The Pioneer

She built homes.

Maria became a pioneering Italian-Canadian real estate broker at a time when women faced barriers entering professional life.

Her work was more than buying and selling property. For families establishing themselves in Ottawa, a home meant stability, opportunity and belonging.

II
The Translator

She built bridges.

As a court translator, Maria helped Italian-speaking members of her community navigate institutions where language could otherwise become a barrier.

She became a bridge between languages, cultures and people.

III
The Advocate

She built community.

Maria managed a prenatal clinic serving Italian women in Ottawa, creating a place where women could receive support during an important moment in their lives.

Her work reflected something larger: a belief that a community becomes stronger when its people care for one another.
