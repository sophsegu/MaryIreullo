The Story

STORY 01

The Pioneer

In 1955, Maria began working as a real estate agent. Two years later, after passing her broker examination, she opened her own office.

Her career made her a pioneer for Italian Canadian women in Ottawa.

STORY 02

The Translator

Maria's command of languages allowed her to serve as a court translator, helping Italian speakers navigate important legal and institutional situations.

STORY 03

The Advocate

Her dedication to community service included organizing prenatal clinics for young Italian women and helping families in Ottawa's Italian community.

STORY 04

The Poet

Behind the professional accomplishments was another side of Maria: a woman who expressed herself through poetry.

Her writing gives future generations another way to hear her voice.
