Bibliography
& Sources

A record of published articles, magazine features, online resources, digitized historical materials, and archival records consulted in documenting Maria Ierullo’s life and contributions.

This bibliography is maintained separately from the Historical Archive. The archive presents selected records with fuller descriptions; this page provides citation details and a direct route to the source where available.

Archival Contribution

City of Ottawa Archives

Archivist: Theresa Sorel

The historical materials relating to Maria Ierullo are being documented and organized through archival work with the City of Ottawa Archives. Archivist Theresa Sorel is contributing archival expertise to the documentation, organization, and contextualization of historical records associated with Maria Ierullo.

This archival work provides professional archival context for the materials referenced throughout this website and supports their preservation as part of Ottawa’s broader historical record.

The archival work is distinct from the editorial content of this website. Historical claims presented here remain attributed to the original records and published sources from which they are drawn.

Maria Ierullo’s Translation and Community Services

Porte, Brian J. “Maria Ierullo’s Translation and Community Services.” Newspaper article, n.d.
